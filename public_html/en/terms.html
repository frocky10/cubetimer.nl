Terms of use

Last updated: 30 September 2026. This is a translation; if the two differ, the Dutch version applies.

In short

  • CubeTimer is free. You can use it to practise, measure your progress and take part in the leaderboards.
  • Times on the leaderboard have to be real solves. Cheating can cost you your place or your account.
  • We do our best, but we can't promise the site always works. Back up your times now and then (you can download them from the account window).

1. The service

CubeTimer (cubetimer.nl) is a free online timer for speedcubers, with scrambles, statistics, leaderboards, challenges, tournaments and a learn page, among other things. By using the site you agree to these terms. How we handle your data is explained in the privacy policy.

The site is paid for with ads. We may add, change or stop features; if it's something big, we'll let you know in good time.

2. Your account

  • One account per person. Choose a password you don't use anywhere else and keep it to yourself; you're responsible for what happens with your account.
  • Choose a username that isn't offensive, discriminatory or misleading and doesn't impersonate someone else (such as a well-known cuber). We may change an inappropriate name.
  • If you're under 16, talk to your parents or guardians before creating an account.
  • You can delete your account yourself at any time from the account window. All your data is then gone immediately.

3. Fair timing

The leaderboards, records, challenges and achievements are only fun if everyone plays fair. So:

  • Only real solves, done by you, with the scramble the site gave you (or an official WCA scramble).
  • Don't make up or edit times, or submit them through the console, a script or any other way than using the timer.
  • No solves by someone else under your name, and no second account to help yourself in duels or leaderboards.

Times that look impossible are flagged automatically and only count once we've reviewed them. If we find cheating, we can remove times from the leaderboards, and block your account if it keeps happening. The times in your own sessions always remain yours.

4. What's not allowed

  • Attacking or overloading the site, or trying to get into places you shouldn't (found a weakness? Report it to privacy@cubetimer.nl and we'll fix it).
  • Using bots or scripts to create accounts, fill in forms or scrape data from the site.
  • Using the feedback button for spam, advertising or messages unrelated to the site.
  • Harassing other users, for example through duels.

5. Who owns what

Your times and your data are yours; you can always download them. You only give us permission to use them for the site itself, such as your name and records on the leaderboards if you take part.

The design, texts and code of CubeTimer are ours. The scrambles come from cubing.js (MPL-2.0), the font is Inter (SIL Open Font License), and the results of past competitions come from the World Cube Association. CubeTimer isn't affiliated with the WCA or with any brand of cubes or smart cubes.

6. No guarantees

CubeTimer is free and provided as is. We do our best to keep the site working, secure and correct, but we can't promise it's always available, never has a bug or never loses data. So download your data as a backup now and then. As far as the law allows, we're not liable for damage caused by using the site, such as lost times. Your rights as a consumer still apply.

You use smart cubes at your own risk; we haven't been able to test the connection with every model ourselves.

7. Stopping

You can stop at any time by deleting your account. We can block or delete an account if someone breaks these terms seriously or repeatedly. If we ever stop running CubeTimer, we'll let you know in good time so you can download your data.

8. Changes and law

We may update these terms; the date at the top shows when that last happened. For important changes we'll let you know on the site. These terms are governed by Dutch law.

9. Contact

Questions about these terms? Email privacy@cubetimer.nl. For a bug or idea you can also use the feedback button on the site.
