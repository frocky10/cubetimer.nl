    Smart cube

    Connect your smart cube

    The timer starts on your first turn after the scramble and stops when the cube is solved.

    • GAN
    • MoYu
    • QiYi
    • X-Man
    • Giiker
    • GoCube

    Wake your cube up (a quick turn is enough) and pick it in the window that opens. Works in Chrome and Edge on Windows, macOS, Android and ChromeOS.

    1. Choose cube
    2. Finding the cube's address
    3. Connect
    4. Reading the cube state

    Where do I find it?
    • In Chrome or Edge on a computer: open chrome://bluetooth-internals, click Start scan and find your cube in the list. The address is shown next to it.
    • In your cube's app: under the device details or settings.
    • Faster next time: turn on chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features in Chrome and the site will read the address itself.

    You only need to enter it once; this browser remembers it after that.
