About CubeTimer

CubeTimer is a free timer for speedcubers, made in the Netherlands. You open the site, press the space bar and time. No installation, no account needed, and it keeps working if your internet drops out for a moment.

Why another timer?

There are good timers already. We were missing one that looks calm, works in Dutch as well as English, and still does everything a dedicated cuber wants: official scrambles, WCA inspection, proper statistics and smart cubes. And we wanted timing itself to be more fun if you want it to be, with leaderboards, a scramble of the day, tournaments, game modes and achievements. If you don't need any of that, all you see is a timer.

What you can do with it

  • Time with the space bar or a tap on your screen, with WCA inspection and automatic +2 and DNF.
  • Official scrambles for all 17 WCA events, made with cubing.js, the same kind as at a competition.
  • Track your times in sessions, with Ao5, Ao12 up to Ao100, a graph and statistics per day and per week.
  • Connect a smart cube (GAN, QiYi, X-Man, MoYu, Giiker, GoCube) and see per solve how long the cross, F2L, OLL and PLL took.
  • Learn: the beginner's method, F2L and every OLL and PLL algorithm on the learn page, and guides on notation, practice, averages and competitions.
  • Measure yourself against others on the leaderboards, in the daily challenges, in tournaments against a simulated field, or at a real past WCA competition against the real times from back then.

How we want to do it

  • Free. The site is paid for by advertising. Ads are only personalised if you agree to that.
  • Your times are yours. Without an account they're only stored in your own browser. With an account you can always download them with one click, and delete your account with everything on it yourself.
  • No requests to third parties without consent. The font and the scramble code are on our own server. Google Analytics only loads once you say yes. How that works exactly is in the privacy policy.
  • Fair leaderboards. Records and averages are calculated on the server from your solves, not in your browser. Times that look impossibly fast only count once someone has reviewed them.

The code

CubeTimer's source code is public on GitHub. There you can see how everything works, from calculating an Ao5 to talking to a smart cube. The list of what's coming next is there too, as issues.

Contact

Found a bug or have an idea? Use the feedback button at the top right of the timer. Your report then arrives as an issue on GitHub, with your browser and screen size included, so we can find the problem faster. Note: those reports are publicly readable, so don't put any personal information in them.

For anything else, and for questions about your data, email privacy@cubetimer.nl. Answers to common questions are in the FAQ.
